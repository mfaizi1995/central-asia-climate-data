About

Central Asia Climate Data is a public portal aimed at making climate indicators easier to find, cite, and use — starting with Afghanistan.

Our Mission

We believe climate data should be accessible to everyone. Our mission is to process, standardize, and distribute satellite-derived climate indicators for Central Asia, making it easier for researchers, policymakers, NGOs, and local organizations to understand and respond to climate challenges in the region.

All data is free to use, with clear documentation of sources and methods. We prioritize transparency, reproducibility, and ease of use.

Project Goals

Accessibility

Provide ready-to-use climate data in simple formats (CSV) that don't require specialized GIS software or programming skills.

Consistency

Use standardized processing methods and administrative boundaries across all countries and indicators for cross-border analysis.

Transparency

Document all data sources, quality filters, and processing steps so users can understand and trust the data they're using.
